Quantum Effect - Master Design & Mechanics Bible
Engine Context: OpenNefia (LÖVE / Lua) | Visuals: 16x16 Pixel Art
1. Core Philosophy & Tone
The Macro (Mass Effect): A vast, awe-inspiring, but deeply melancholic multiverse. The scale is interstellar and inter-dimensional.
The Micro (Cyberpunk 2077): Gritty, street-level survival. High tech, low life. Absolute freedom to shape the world, commit atrocities, or become a savior.
The "Limbo" Rule: The Multiverse is not a "take-two" or a way to fix mistakes. It is a graveyard of "what-ifs." Parallel universes are distant yet familiar. NPCs react to their sealed fates differently: some become nihilistic Technopunks, some fight to the bitter end, some bury their heads in the sand. The world feels alive through this varied, tragic realism.

3. The Namesake Mechanic: "Quantum Effect"
The "Quantum Effect" represents the scientifically unexplainable phenomena caused by Dark Matter/Quantum energy. In gameplay, it manifests as a dual-state risk/reward system tied to the player's exposure to the Quantum Limbo.
A. Quantum Corruption (The Debuff / Degradation)
Trigger: Exposure to "Quantum Storms" (Etherwind) in the Limbo or deep ruins.
Effect: Acts like Elona's Ether Disease. Slowly mutates the Quantum Baby, causing stat debuffs, visual glitches, and eventually permanent physical/mental penalties if not treated with "Stabilizers" (Potions).
Engine Hook: Use the native ether and mutation data tables.
B. Quantumize (The Buff / Overclocking)
Trigger: Intentionally installing unstable Bio-Mechanical Implants or using "Overclock" protocols.
Effect: Grants massive, reality-bending buffs (extra turns, massive damage, hacking without compute). However, it is unstable. It acts like a "poison" or "curse" that continuously drains HP, Energy, or sanity until the player rests or uses "Coolants".
Engine Hook: Use the native buff/debuff and equipment systems.
4. Factions & "Gods" (Willpower in a Machine World)
In a world reliant on machines, willpower and ideology are the only true "divine" forces.
The Mechanic: We map Elona's 8 Gods to our major Factions (e.g., Quantum Revolution, Aurelian Order, Void Seekers, Synthic Faith).
Gameplay: "Praying at an altar" becomes "Synchronizing with a Faction Beacon" or "Meditating on an Ideology". This grants temporary "Blessings" (Faction-specific buffs).
Reputation: Acts exactly like Elona's God relations, but tied to Faction reputation. If you anger the Aurelian Order, they withhold their "blessings" and send assassins.
5. Bio-Mechanical Augmentations & Mutations
The Quantum Baby is a canvas for modification.
Slots: We expand the native equipment slots. Head (Neuro), Arms/Legs (Limbs), Chest (Torso/Core), Back/Spine (Spinal).
Gameplay: Installing an augmentation is equipping an item. However, to simulate "rejection" or "instability", heavy augmentations increase the player's "Quantum Weight" (Encumbrance) or trigger the Quantumize effect (draining HP for stats).
Engine Hook: Extend the base.item prototype to include an is_augment = true flag, and use the equip event callbacks to apply the stat modifiers.
6. Day-to-Day Loop & Economy
The Grind: Scavenging pre-collapse ruins (dungeons) for tech; running smuggling routes through the Quantum Limbo (overworld); hunting bounties for the Vagabonds or Eclipse Syndicate.
The Economy:
Credits: Standard currency for basic goods, bribes, and faction dues.
Quantum Shards / Data: Rare currency dropped by high-tier enemies or found in deep ruins. Used exclusively for high-end Bio-mechanical augmentations and ship upgrades.
Absolute Freedom: The engine allows killing any NPC. If you turn a hub city into a slaughterhouse, the local faction's reputation drops to "Enemy", locking you out of their shops and triggering bounty hunters. The world reacts, but it does not stop.
7. The Multiverse & The Quantum Limbo
The Limbo (Overworld): The space between universes. Navigated via the repurposed Elona overworld map. Moving tile-by-tile represents interstellar/inter-dimensional travel. Random encounters here are "Void Anomalies" or "Pirates".
Hub Zones (Maps/Towns): Planets, Faction HQs, and Cities are disconnected InstancedMap clusters.
Quantum Warp: The transition between the Limbo and a Hub. Handled by a custom UI Layer that plays a distortion effect and loads the target map.
