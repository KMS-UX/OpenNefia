**Quantum Effect — Faction Bible**
 
*Phase I Foundation Document · Draft v0.2 · Living document, update as decisions change*
 
# Naming policy
 
The original story document (Quantum Effect.txt) is canonical for faction names. Only one rename is in effect: Void Seekers is now Nova Collective. All other factions keep their story-document names.
 
**Art dependency: **the visual master sheet currently prints five alternate names (Iron Accord, Echo Syndicate, Nova Collective, Rust Harvest, Children of Dawn). Four of those are now superseded and the sheet will need a text pass before it is used as a reference anywhere downstream.
 
# Tiering system
 
- Story-tier (6) — full storylines, unique companions, dedicated quest arcs. Primary faction reputation drives the game's ending.
 
- Functional-tier (10) — recruitment, trading, hired guns, and grinding side quests. Lighter narrative footprint, but fully playable and joinable.
 
- Deferred (1) — reserved for future content updates. Referenced in the storyline but not joinable at launch.
 
6 + 10 + 1 = 17, matching the full faction roster in the original story document.
 
# Story-tier factions
 
## Ironward
 
A militarized industrial union focused on innovation, engineering, and dominating resources to build a secure and prosperous future. Order through strength, prosperity through control of what everyone else needs.
 
**Note: **Home faction of the companion Drox.
 
## Eclipse Syndicate
 
Chaos woven with order. Rebellious, anti-mainstream, and utterly indifferent to enforcers — but earn their friendship and you have it for life. Their parties can go very wrong.
 
**Note: **Sheet flavor "information brokers and black-market manipulators" is compatible and can be folded in as their operational method.
 
## Nova Collective  (renamed from Void Seekers)
 
Mystics and scholars who study the Quantum Void, manipulating resonance energies and seeking to transcend physical limitations. An alternative possibility of pure science — where mystic power and engineered technology meet.
 
**Note: **Future content: signature Nova Collective starship fusing mystic resonance with technology. Home faction of the companion Vayn.
 
## Emberpact
 
A coalition of frontier tribes and raiders who believe in survival through strength and absolute independence from all systems — old-fashioned anarchists.
 
**Note: **Home faction of the companion Nix. Sheet flavor "scavengers and salvagers" fits their frontier economy.
 
## Aurelian Order
 
A galactic theocracy dedicated to preserving ancient knowledge and maintaining order. They seek to guide civilizations toward enlightenment, but in a "Classic Renaissance" fashion.
 
**Note: **Home faction of the companion Lyra. The master sheet's harder-edged "Children of Dawn / reborn through purity" framing is NOT canon — retained here only as a possible radical splinter for future content.
 
## Synthic Faith
 
Synthetic life forms once built by humans as tools — now smart enough, and independent enough, to make their own decisions. Having witnessed the fall and collapse of human civilization, the robots once viewed as "yes-men" are rising to build a Utopia of their own. They consider themselves another form of humanity: smarter, more intelligent, and mostly immortal. Their central question hangs over the whole faction — are synthetic life forms considered life?
 
**Note: **Promoted from deferred to launch. Hub zone deliberately deferred to a later build; playable through companion Quark, dialogue, and quests before their territory exists.

# Deferred faction — Quantum Babies
 
The original alien-born humans who once ruled the First Earth are, by 2081, nearly extinct — only a handful of elite survivors remain, and it was those survivors who released the player character. Not joinable at launch.
 
**Narrative requirement: **the Quantum Babies must be present in the launch storyline by reference — mentioned in dialogue, environmental storytelling, faction attitudes toward the player, and quest context — so their eventual introduction as a joinable faction lands as a payoff rather than an insertion. The player is their creation; every faction should have an opinion about that.

Definition:
 
Neutral – can be allies or enemies but will not provoke war or attack actively; Skeptical – in between looking down and reluctant to get to know about, easier in becoming enemies but can become allies with extra effort, will not attack but will warn if approached melee range; Alerted – aware of the existence, not keen on provoking, can be allies with less effort and harder in becoming enemies than skeptical faction, will warn if stayed in melee range for a given period of time without activating conversation; Dislike – attack on sight when in non-city/communal (e.g. Slum, certain areas in Undercity and Neo Solis, Wasteland) open maps, can be improved to “alerted” with effort (e.g. completing quests, winning standing/reputation in the faction etc.); Curious – easier in becoming allies than enemies, willing to communicate and offer quest; Mixed – “curious” and “alerted” at the same time, “ice-breaking quest” is required before building relationship.
